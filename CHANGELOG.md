## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added

- The upcoming blood drives of the Établissement français du sang (EFS), read from its public map
  (no account, no key) every 12 hours.
- One device, the zone (around the house located in Gladys or around a point entered, 5 to 50 km),
  with the number of drive days in the next 7 and 30 days and the next drive: place, day and hours,
  distance, online appointment and free slots.
- "Blood drives" dashboard widget: the next drives of the zone, with a link to book.
- Scene triggers "New blood drive announced", "Blood drive today" and "Blood drive tomorrow", and
  the scene action "Nearby blood drives" (one sentence).
- Texts and documentation in English, French, German and Spanish.
